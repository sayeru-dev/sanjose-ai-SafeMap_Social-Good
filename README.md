# SJSU SafeMap: AI for Campus Safety Reporting and Awareness

Client: City of San José, campus safety  
Team: Taran, Rithvik, James, & Eric Aug–Oct 2026  

My part: I helped build the interactive campus safety heat map and reporting interface.

## The problem

Students, staff, residents, and visitors may have difficulty understanding where safety concerns are being reported around campus. Without a centralized visual system, it is harder to recognize patterns and determine where additional attention may be needed.

## What we built

- An interactive campus map that displays approved report activity by location and intensity.
- A reporting interface with separate options for SJSU students/staff and residents/visitors.
- A prototype workflow where analysts and campus personnel review reports and decide what action to take.

## What I would do next

I would connect the prototype to a verified, privacy-protected incident database and test the map and reporting process with actual campus users.
